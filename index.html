Welcome to StudyNest!
Your personal student command centre. Let's take a quick 30-second tour so you know where everything is.
⚡
Dashboard
Your home screen. See today's classes, active reminders, grades summary and attendance — all at a glance.
💡 Tip: The stats cards at the top auto-update every time you add data elsewhere in the app.
🗓️
Schedule & Attendance
Add your class timetable under My Schedule, then mark attendance daily in the Attendance calendar.
💡 Tip: Click any date on the calendar to toggle present / absent / late.
📈
Grades & Notes
Log test scores under Grades to see subject averages, and keep everything you learn in Smart Notes.
💡 Tip: Turn on the Grades widget from ⚙️ Widgets to see your averages right on the dashboard.

Last updated: March 2026

1. What We Collect

StudyNest collects: your name, email address, username, class/grade, and any academic data you add (notes, grades, attendance, reminders). All data is stored locally in your browser using localStorage — it never leaves your device unless you explicitly export it.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to power StudyNest features — displaying your schedule, calculating attendance, showing grades, etc. We do not sell, share or transmit your personal data to any third party.

3. Data Storage

All data is stored in your browser's localStorage. This means: (a) data is tied to your browser/device, (b) clearing browser data will erase your StudyNest data, (c) your data is not backed up to any server unless you use an integration that explicitly says so.

4. Your Rights

You can delete all your data at any time from Profile → Data Management → Clear All Data. This permanently removes your account and all associated data from this device.
